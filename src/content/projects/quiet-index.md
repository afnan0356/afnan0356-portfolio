**Demonstration content.** This entry exists to show the three non-live project states the
template supports:

| Status | Meaning | Card treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `live` | Publicly deployed and working | Green dot, "Live" |
| `in-progress` | Being built, not ready to show | Amber dot, "In progress" |
| `completed` | Finished, may not be online | Green dot, "Completed" |
| `archived` | No longer maintained | Grey dot, "Archived" |

Nothing here is presented as finished work — which is the point. The archive is allowed to
contain experiments, and saying so honestly is more useful than inflating a portfolio.
