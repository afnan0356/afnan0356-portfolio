There is a version of "learning in public" that is mostly theatre: the screenshot of a terminal, the
progress thread, the confident summary of a topic two days into studying it. It reads well and
teaches nothing.

The version worth doing is narrower. Publish the artefact, not the performance:

- **A working thing**, however small, with a link someone can click.
- **A decision**, with the reasoning that led to it and what you would do differently.
- **A question you genuinely do not know the answer to**, stated plainly.

What it excludes is the part that feels most rewarding to write: the narrative of your own
progress. Nobody needs that. What they need is the thing you made and the honest sentence about
where it currently stands.
