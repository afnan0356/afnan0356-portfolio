01 / What is built

Publish without a backend

Markdown and MDX collections drive project pages, articles, standalone writing, book overviews and chapters. Tags and document categories generate their own archives.

A coherent personal site

Shared navigation, about and contact pages, a printable CV, a custom 404, generated social image, bundled fonts and responsive CSS.

Honest defaults

Empty CV fields stay empty. Missing email and PDF links are not fabricated. Samples are labelled and excluded from the sitemap; drafts are excluded from production pages.

Progressive enhancement

Light/dark theme initialization, reduced-motion styles, skip navigation and no-JavaScript-safe content visibility are implemented. They still require browser validation.
